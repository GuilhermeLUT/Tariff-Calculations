This GitHub repository allows the analysis of the Finnish Distribution System Operator (DSO) medium-voltage (MV) grid tariff structure and offers the possibility for an annual cost comparison between the DSO areas in Finland. 
The data is screened from all 81 operating DSOs, out of which 66 are open for public industries and have shared their grid tariffs online.
For each row, the dataset lists all available pricing schemes for electricy consumption as well as the Battery Energy Storage System (BESS) connection.
To showcase the price distributions between DSO areas, a case study was conducted. In the case study, a single Heavy-Duty Charging Station (HDCS) installation was proposed to allow an assessment of the costs of a full year of operation in each DSO area. Here, the least costly pricing scheme within each DSO area was selected to the analysis.
The results from this case study suggest that the location of HDCS will have a significant effect on the operational costs, varying between X - Y thousand euros annually, with the median cost amounting to Z euros. This indicates that the grid tariffs should be considered as part of the decision-making and planning phases to minimize the grid costs to the industry operator.
However, these are the results of a single preliminary case study, and consequently, similar results with other cases or parameters are not guaranteed. For different or more specific industry cases, the authors suggest making an individual analysis for each case to derive reliable results.
